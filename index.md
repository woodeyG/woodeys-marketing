# Woodey's Place

**Your private home cinema.**

Welcome to Woodey’s Place, built to bring the authentic box office experience right to your home theater.

## What it is

Woodey's Place lets your partner, family, or friends actually reserve their seats and book a night at your cinema, turning a simple movie night into a proper event.

## Features

- **Book the night** — Pick a showtime, choose who's coming, snacks and seats included — and get a proper ticket stub, right down to "ADMIT ONE" or "ADMIT TWO."
- **Now Showing** — A rotating hero banner puts your featured picks front and centre, trailers and all.
- **Know where to look** — Tag every film with where you'd actually go to watch it — NAS, physical media, your Apple TV library — so you're never hunting for the disc.
- **Track everything** — Mark it watched, rate it, write a few notes. A running history of every night, together.
- **Your stats** — Movie nights, hours watched, favourite format and genre, even which day of the week you watch the most.
- **Shared, privately** — Invite through iCloud — no accounts, no passwords. Everyone sees the same library, in sync, in real time.

## Private by design

Everything lives in your own iCloud account. No ads, no tracking, no data collected beyond what you put in yourself.
